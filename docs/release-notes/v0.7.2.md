## 新功能 · What's new

- 🔢 **剪贴板的序号标在图标上**：前九条的图标角上一直标着序号，`Alt+1`～`Alt+9` 直接粘贴第几条，加按 `Shift` 粘贴为纯文本，不用先按住 `Alt` 等它出来。设置里可以改成 `Ctrl` 或关掉。
  **Clipboard numbers on the icons**: the first nine rows always show their number on the icon; `Alt+1`…`Alt+9` pastes that row, with `Shift` as plain text, without holding `Alt` first. Settings can switch it to `Ctrl` or turn it off.
- 🖼️ **图片缩略图更大**：图片那一行更高，一张 16:10 的截图整张都看得到。
  **Bigger picture thumbnails**: a picture's row is taller, and a whole 16:10 screenshot fits.
- 📋 **图中的文字可以复制**：预览里识别出的文字可以用鼠标选中，按 `Ctrl+C` 或点“复制选中”复制选中的部分，点“复制文字”复制全部；复制的内容也会进剪贴板历史。
  **Copy the words in a picture**: the words read from a picture can be selected in the preview; `Ctrl+C` or “Copy selection” copies the selection, “Copy text” all of it, and the copy goes into the history too.
- ⌨️ **剪贴板底栏更清爽**：底栏只留常用的键（Enter、`Alt+1`～`9`，有地方时还有 Tab 和 →），其余快捷键放进右下角的 ⓘ 按钮，鼠标停上去就能看到，和搜索框的一样。
  **A tidier clipboard footer**: the footer keeps the keys used most (Enter, `Alt+1`…`9`, and Tab and → where there is room); the rest are behind the ⓘ button at the bottom right, shown when the mouse rests on it, as in the search window.
